Key Findings

1. Calculated Energy (V×I) Always Exceeds Tuya Reading

The Calculated Energy uses P = V × I (Apparent Power in VA) without Power Factor correction.
The Tuya Smart Plug measures Real Power (W) with built-in PF compensation.
This means Calculated Energy is always higher than actual energy consumed.

2. Power Factor Varies Significantly

Reliable readings (PF 0.82 - 0.95):
  • MG ZS EV 16A → PF = 0.950 (best accuracy)
  • MG ZS EV 8A → PF = 0.900 (typical EV charger)
  • RD6 Riddara 16A → PF = 0.819
Suspicious readings (PF < 0.72):
  • RD6 Riddara 10A → PF = 0.544 (likely Tuya reading error)
  • RD6 Riddara 13A → PF = 0.455 (likely Tuya reading error)
  • MG ZS EV 10A → PF = 0.714
  • MG ZS EV 13A → PF = 0.695
Normal EV charger PF should be 0.85 - 0.95. Values below 0.7 suggest the Tuya meter may have low resolution (0.01 kWh steps) or update lag at lower currents.

3. 16A is the Most Efficient & Reliable

  • MG ZS EV at 16A: PF = 0.950, only 5% difference between Calc and Tuya
  • RD6 Riddara at 16A: PF = 0.819, 18% difference
  • 16A delivers ~3.0 kWh/hr real energy — about 2x more than 8A
  • At 16A, charging a 40 kWh battery from 20% to 100% takes only ~10.6 hours

4. 10A and 13A Show Anomalous Tuya Data (RD6)

For RD6 Riddara sessions at 10A and 13A, the Tuya Delta is suspiciously low:
  • 10A: Tuya reported only 1.25 kWh over 60 min, but calculated is 2.30 kWh
  • 13A: Tuya reported only 1.36 kWh over 60 min, but calculated is 2.99 kWh
This is likely a Tuya Smart Plug measurement issue — at lower currents the plug may not sample fast enough or the 0.01 kWh resolution causes significant rounding errors over short intervals. The Calculated Energy values at these current levels are more reliable.

5. Cost Efficiency

At Thailand's off-peak rate of 4.72 THB/kWh:
  • 8A: ~7 THB/hr (slowest, ~23 hr to full charge)
  • 10A: ~6-8 THB/hr (unreliable Tuya data)
  • 13A: ~6-9 THB/hr (unreliable Tuya data)
  • 16A: ~14 THB/hr (best value — charges in ~10.6 hr, PF highest)
Recommendation: Charge at 16A during off-peak hours for best efficiency and shortest charge time.

Formula Reference

Apparent Power (VA)   = V × I
Real Power (W)        = V × I × PF
Energy (kWh)           = Σ (P × Δt / 3600)
Calculated Energy     = Σ (V × I / 1000 × Δt / 3600)
Tuya Raw Energy       = DPS point '1' / 100 (cumulative, normalized to 0)
Power Factor           = Tuya Delta / Calculated Energy
